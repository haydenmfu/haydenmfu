# Hey, I’m Hayden 👋

I’m a **Computer Science + Statistics student at Cornell University** who likes building tools for problems that are too messy to fit neatly into a benchmark.

My work sits at the intersection of **machine learning, environmental systems, policy data, AI evaluation, and cybersecurity**. I’m especially interested in the space where good software meets careful measurement: satellite observations, weather sensors, long-horizon forecasts, public datasets, and the people affected by the systems we model.

## What I’m working on

- **Water systems:** particle-based inference for estimating unobserved irrigation from satellite and land-surface data
- **AI research:** probing representation drift and forecast instability in long-horizon LLM agents
- **Climate + sensing:** correctional models for lake-effect snow forecasting with field-collected sensor data
- **Responsible AI:** studying political bias and fairness in language models and predictive systems
- **Research software:** building maps and data tools that make cultural, historical, and scientific resources easier to explore

## A few things I care about

- Research should be reproducible, legible, and useful outside the paper abstract.
- The best projects usually cross boundaries: software and statistics, models and fieldwork, technical rigor and human context.
- Messy data is not a nuisance to hide; it is often the most interesting part of the problem.
- Security and fairness are engineering requirements, not final-stage decorations.
